# FHB-2 · why the RZZ/RX brickwork is not a matchgate circuit

A Lean 4 correctness witness for the FHB-2 benchmark claim in
[`AwareLiquid/The-Fuxi-Hypercube-Math`](https://github.com/AwareLiquid/The-Fuxi-Hypercube-Math).

## The claim under review

FHB-2 is described as

> "a nearest-neighbor **RZZ/RX** brickwork on a line — a **matchgate
> (free-fermion) circuit** whose ⟨Xⱼ⟩ / ⟨XⱼXₖ⟩ references are exactly computable
> in O(n²·d) via Majorana covariance evolution, at any depth and any n."

The covariance route requires every generator to be at most **quadratic** in the
Majorana operators.  Under Jordan–Wigner (indices from 1),

```
γ_{2l−1} = (∏_{i<l} Z_i) X_l ,      γ_{2l} = (∏_{i<l} Z_i) Y_l ,
```

| generator | Majorana form | degree | Gaussian? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Z_l` | `−i γ_{2l−1} γ_{2l}` | 2 | yes |
| `i X_l X_{l+1}` | `γ_{2l} γ_{2l+1}` | 2 | yes |
| `X_1` | `γ_1` | 1 | yes, after a `(2n+1)` extension |
| `X_l`, `l ≥ 2` | `−i γ_1 γ_2 … γ_{2l−1}` | `2l−1` | **no** |

## What is formalised here

Explicit 4×4 complex matrices on two qubits, with `γ₁ = X⊗1`, `γ₂ = Y⊗1`,
`γ₃ = Z⊗X`, `γ₄ = Z⊗Y`:

```lean
theorem clifford (μ ν : Fin 4) :
    g μ * g ν + g ν * g μ = (if μ = ν then 2 else 0) • 1

theorem X2_cubic : X2 = -Complex.I • (g 0 * g 1 * g 2)      -- degree 3, NOT Gaussian
theorem Z2_quadratic : Z2 = -Complex.I • (g 2 * g 3)        -- degree 2, Gaussian
```

`X2_cubic` is the falsification witness: `X` on the second qubit is a **cubic**
Majorana monomial, so it lies outside `span{1, γ_μ, γ_μγ_ν}` and outside the
enlarged `span{1, c_0, γ_μ, c_0γ_μ, γ_μγ_ν}` used to absorb *linear* terms.

## Supporting literature

* **Wan, Huggins, Lee, Babbush**, *Matchgate Shadows for Fermionic Quantum
  Simulation*, Comm. Math. Phys. (2023): matchgates are generated by
  `exp(iθ X⊗X)` and **single-qubit `Z` rotations**, "along with the Pauli `X`
  operator `Xₙ` on the last qubit" — that last-qubit `Xₙ` is the reflection
  lifting `SO(2n)` to `O(2n)`, not a general `X` rotation.
* **Matchgate synthesis** (arXiv:2602.05425): the matchgate group is generated by
  `R^z(θ) = e^{iθZ/2}` and `R^{xx}(θ) = e^{iθX⊗X/2}`.
* **Brod** (arXiv:1207.2126): matchgates include *all single-qubit `Z`-rotations*
  and become universal when supplemented by `H`.
* **Jozsa, Miyake**: arbitrary single-qubit gates are simulable **on the first
  line only**; deeper linear terms "generally act across all the first `k` lines".
* **arXiv:2410.10068**, Theorem 6: the enlarged `(2n+1)` covariance matrix covers
  "**quadratic and linear** products of Jordan–Wigner Majoranas" — linear, not
  string-carrying.

## Repair

Replacing `RX` by `RZ` makes the brickwork a genuine matchgate circuit: `RZ` is
quadratic on every line, so the `O(n²d)` covariance evolution is exact and no
enlargement is needed.  This keeps FHB-2's intent — a nearest-neighbour brickwork
blind to single-qubit `Z` error and sensitive to `ZZ` error.  See
[`docs/FHB2-CORRECTION.md`](https://github.com/everest-an/fhb-spectral-lean/blob/main/docs/FHB2-CORRECTION.md)
for the four repair options.
